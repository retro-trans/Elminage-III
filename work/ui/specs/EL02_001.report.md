# EL02_001 - town facility and facility-action buttons

Spec: work/ui/specs/EL02_001.json. Preview: work/ui/preview/EL02_001.png.

Texts found: 23 labels x 3 states = 69 sprites. Translated: 69 (23 distinct strings). Nothing else on the sheet (opaque area ends at y 457).

## Layout
- Button 114x18 at (114*state, 20*row), states: x0 selected (blue), x114 normal (dark), x228 disabled (dim).
- Columns 0-11 (left curl) and 109-113 (right cap), and rows 16-17 (bottom border) are identical in every row and are never touched.
- The right-hand swirl (x 89-108) is the same in every row; the original draws long labels over it. The English does the same.

## Method
- Reference: row 11 (鍛冶, shortest label). Its text (x 13-42, rows 0-15) is replaced by the same row's plain body 42 px to the right (`blit:X+55,220`). The body is horizontally uniform apart from a faint dither.
- Every other row: x 12-108, rows 0-15 blitted from the cleaned reference. This also resets the swirl. Verified: after erasing, every row equals the reference pixel for pixel, except for the original per-row noise on the dark line at row 16 (max diff 12/1020, not text).
- Text: 16px game font, left-aligned, ink starting at x+14 (the Japanese starts at x+15), dy -1. With dy 0 the outline covered the bottom border line, as it does in the EL01_000 example. Fit width is 91 px, so ink ends at or before x+104. The font order is 16/sp1, then 16/sp0. The 12px font was tried and rejected: it looks bold and is no narrower.
- Colours sampled from the original text:
  - selected: ink 255,255,255, outline 60,6,107
  - normal: ink 220,240,241, outline 12,15,25
  - disabled: ink 61,61,61, outline 4,6,8

## Open points
- Abbreviations that differ from the glossary: Kembeck's (Kembeck's Shop), Alch. Storage (Alchemy Storage), Uncurse/Fix (Uncurse/Repair).
- Algrantz Castle uses a narrower word gap than the font's space. Check it at 1x in game.
- Mucha's Tavern, Call Companions, Algrantz Castle and Cure/Retrieve reach x+104, about 2 px further right than the longest Japanese label. They stay inside the body, before the right cap.
- Help text has both "standby" (HELP_TEXT.json, review_g2) and "have them wait" (csvt slice_06) for 待機. "Stand By" follows the reviewed version.
