# EL03_003 - town place / topic / option-tab buttons

Spec: work/ui/specs/EL03_003.json. Preview: work/ui/preview/EL03_003.png.

Texts found: 26 distinct Japanese texts on 76 sprites: 24 labels x 3 states, 光を宿す x 3 states, and the フェイム caption. Translated: 76/76.
Untouched: row y380 MILL.GP (Latin only, no Japanese), the empty dark bars in column x342 (y60-y300), and the two ornament bars below the Fame caption.

## Layout
- 114x18 plates. Columns: selected x0, normal x114, disabled x228. Rows y = 20*r (r 0-24). This is the same plate art as EL02_002 group A.
- Column x342, y0/y20/y40: 光を宿す in 3 states, stacked vertically. The selected plate matches group A up to rel x109. The normal and disabled plates have their own swirl and noise from rel x71, so only rel x12-70 is replaced there (no visible seam at 6x zoom).
- Fame caption: free-standing text at (344,318) on the transparent sheet background, no plate.

## Method
- Reference plate: Pray (row y420). The selected reference is cleaned with `copy:68`. The normal and disabled references are cleaned by blitting texture from the same plate (rel 51-89 -> 12-50).
- Every other plate gets rel x12-113 (all 18 rows) blitted from the reference of its state. Cols 0-11 hold no text.
- Text: 16 px game font, left-aligned from rel x13, 1 px outline. Colours were sampled from this sheet's glyphs and match EL02_002: selected 255,255,255 / outline 60,6,107; normal 220,240,241 / 13,15,27; disabled 61,61,61 / 6,5,10.
- Width: the swirl starts at rel x90. Labels up to 75 px stay left of it. Longer labels run over the swirl to at most rel x109, the right notch edge, as the original Japanese did (ロスカディル宮殿, ジャコビス術法堂 and フェイス/スタイル設定 reach rel x107-110). No pixel is drawn into the transparent notch.
- Tight labels: labels wider than 96 px at spacing 1 use spacing 0. At spacing 0 the pairs l-i, i-l, a-l and the like merge ("Roscadl", "Mafdsaim"). So each pair with i, l, I, j, ', . or 1 keeps a 1 px gap, and the word space is narrowed to fit. In the spec these labels are split into segments, each its own label with `"label"` (the full English) and `"part"` keys. A gap of at least 1 px between segments means no outline overwrites a neighbour's fill.
  - Toner's Armory: space 5. Central Council, Jakobis Sp. Hall, Highway Market: space 5. Roscadil Palace: space 4.
  - Temple of Rafa: space 3. Mafdsalim House: space 3, starts at rel x12.
  - Star Lighthouse: space 2, starts at rel x12. This is the tightest label, but still readable.
- Fame: 12 px font, spacing 0, colour 251,221,201 (sampled from the original), no outline. The old glyphs are erased with the background's own values (0,0,0,0 for x342-348, 2,0,0,4 after that). The bar mark at x340-341 is kept.

## Abbreviations / open points
- Jakobis Sp. Hall: glossary has Jakobis Spell Hall.
- Star Lighthouse: glossary has Stargazing Lighthouse. The in-game PLACE_NM name display already uses "Star Lighthouse", so the button matches what players see at the top-right.
- Weather Ctrl: glossary has weather control.
- Face/Style: shortened from "Face/Style Settings".
- ロスカディル宮殿 (button) and ロスカディル王宮 (PLACE_NM) both become "Roscadil Palace". That looks right, since they seem to be the same place.
- Star Lighthouse (space 2) and Mafdsalim House (space 3) have narrow word spaces. Check them on hardware or in the emulator for readability at native size.
