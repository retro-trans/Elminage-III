Method
- Buttons: 114x18 cells, 3 columns (x 0/114/228 = selected/normal/disabled), rows at y 0..100 step 20 (same art as EL01_000).
  The shortest row (Book of the Next Age, y100) is cleaned with copy:x0+70 over x0+13..66, then x0+13..109 of every other row is
  blitted from it. Text: 16px font left-aligned at x0+13; selected white / outline (60,6,107); normal (220,240,241) / outline (8,9,11);
  disabled (60,60,60), no outline (colours sampled from the JP glyphs). Spacing 1, falls back to 0 for the two long labels.
- Display toggles (Show/Translucent/Hide): 48x12 plates at x 120/168/216, y 154 (normal, black) and 166 (selected, red).
  Plate 1 centre cleaned by copying the dark centre columns (x132 / x154) inward, top/bottom border rows re-copied, then plate 1
  blitted to plates 2-3. 12px font, white with dark (2,0,1) outline, centred (JP glyphs are 9px tall, like the 12px caps).
- BGM category tags (Sound Load slot labels, = BGM_NAME.CSV column 4): 40x16 grid from y177, interior 38x14 at (40c+1, Y+2).
  Interiors blitted from the empty cell (121,243). 16px font, colour (255,239,220), no outline, centred, caps fill the 14px interior
  like the JP. Descenders (p, y, g) reach the frame row Y+16; pseudo-labels (jp empty, en " ") re-blit that row from the clean bar
  at (121,257), so the frame stays intact and descenders are clipped by one row.

Untouched (no Japanese): PERSON plate, Export/Import plates, VOL strips, up/down arrows, the ED and ??? tags, empty tag cells,
orbs/ring/figures, empty dark bars.

Open points
- "Next Age Book" is a shortening of the item name "Book of the Next Age"; at 94px it covers the right-hand swirl (the JP was short).
  Alternative if that looks busy in game: "Next Age".
- Tag cells hold at most 38px of 16px text, so several names are abbreviated (Town1, Pub, Tmpl., Train., Adv1-8, Btl1-6) and
  "Castle", "Town1-4", "Rowdy", "Doubt", "Happy" fill the cell edge to edge.
- 酒場 tag uses "Pub" (synonym) because "Tavern" does not fit; glossary place names keep "Tavern".
- Palette quantisation maps ~730 anti-aliased tag-text pixels to an existing palette colour with alpha 248 (vs 255); invisible in practice.
- The cleaned button background has a flat bottom bevel row under the text (as in EL01_000), visible as a faint line between words at zoom.
