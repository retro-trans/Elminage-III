Japanese texts found: 71. Translated: 71. Unreadable: 0. Not fitted at full length: 3 (abbreviated, see table).

## Method
- **Battle buttons** (76x19 cells, columns x 0/76/152 = selected/normal/disabled, rows y 0..120 step 20) and **Ambush** (x 228/304/380, y 0): the reference row (Attack / Ambush, two kanji) is cleaned with `copy:` of a clean column (x+44) over x+11..x+39, rows y+2..y+16. The other six battle rows then get their whole text area (x+11..x+63, which includes the right-hand cap) by `blit:` from the cleaned reference row. 16px font, left-aligned at x+12, glyph top at y+2 (the same as the source). Colours: selected white with a (97,48,35) outline; normal (242,231,223) with a dark (18,12,11) outline; disabled (72,71,71). Attack and Defend use spacing 0 (50-51px at spacing 1 would run into the cap). The others use spacing 1.
- **Tarot / Barrier plates** (x 228/304/380, y 20 and 40): these are alpha-mask sprites. RGB is near-black everywhere, the plate is translucent (alpha around 216 with a vertical gradient) and the text is opaque (alpha around 250). Barrier row: cleaned with `copy:` (x+45) over x+5..x+35, and its glyph halo in the gap row y39 is cleared with `fill:0,0,0,0`. Tarot row: blitted from the cleaned Barrier row (same plate shape, including the right-hand swirl). The English is drawn black as two `text` entries 1px apart (faux bold, to match the bold source glyphs) with glyph top y+22 / y+42.
- **Disclaimer plates**: big plate (7..421, y163..176) cleaned with `copy:172`. "Disclaimer" uses the 16px font, centred, glyph top y162. A pseudo-label (`en " "`, `bg copy:172`) then restores the top frame row y163, which the 16px ascenders would cover. Small plate (y187..198): `copy:186`, then the 12px font, centred.
- **Chest action buttons** (95x18, columns x 0/96/192, rows y 260..360 step 20): the reference row Open (y340) is cleaned with `copy:` (x+57) over x+14..x+56. The other rows get x+13..x+83 blitted from it, which restores the left part of the right-hand swirl that long JP labels covered. 16px, left at x+14, glyph top y+1. Colours: selected white with a (80,40,130) outline; normal (220,240,241) with a (9,13,15) outline; disabled (60,60,60).
- **Servant-bag menu buttons** (114x18, columns x 0/114/228, rows y 396..476 step 20): same method as the chest buttons. The reference row is Servant's Bag (y436): `copy:` x+67 over x+14..x+65, then a blit of x+13..x+103 to the other rows. Same colours as the chest buttons.
- **Small bag plates** (x 289..365 and 368..447, rows y 260/276/292): each plate is cleaned with `copy:` (x357 / x375) over its interior rows y+2..y+13. The text uses the 12px font, centred, in the source colours: (254,231,170) on the teal plate, (240,211,175) on the brown plate, (60,60,60) disabled.
- 16px descenders (p, q, g, y) on the chest and servant-bag buttons reach the 1px dark frame line at y+16, as the source glyphs nearly do. I left them uncut.
- The following contain no Japanese and were left untouched: the red and blue gradient bars (x 228..455, y 60..139), the skull/status icon strip (y 380..394) and every ornament.

## Open points
- **Tool limitation (Font12):** `tools/img_text.py` `Font12` draws the FONT12_12 glyph alpha only. In that font the alpha covers both the white letter and its black outline, so 12px text comes out as filled "blob" letters with the counters closed (affects the small Disclaimer plate and the Svt. Bag / Myst. Bag plates here, and the 12px labels in EL01_002). The labels are readable at 1x but look heavier than the source. Fix suggestion: the letter mask should be alpha where RGB is white; draw the black part as an optional outline. The 12px entries in this spec are `text` entries with `font: "12"`, so they would pick up such a fix on rebuild with no spec change.
- **Tool/doc mismatch:** label-level `fonts` is documented but ignored. `build_entry` only reads `style.fonts`. For that reason this spec sets `style.fonts = [["16",1],["16",0]]` and uses `text` entries (explicit font and spacing) for the 12px texts and the alpha-mask Tarot/Barrier texts.
- Tarot/Barrier plates: in-game tinting of these alpha-mask sprites is unknown. The English copies the source contrast (opaque text on a translucent plate). Check in battle.
- Chest buttons: Disarm Trap / Spell Unlock overlap the left edge of the right-hand swirl by about 7px (the JP overlapped it by about 5px).
